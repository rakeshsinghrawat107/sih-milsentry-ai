STRAD: Spatio-Temporal Relay Anomaly Detection

Attackers inject fake intermediate headers to pretend an email came from a trusted server. We apply the laws of physics:

Δt_observed < (2 × Distance(A, B) / c) + Overhead ⟹ FORGED HEADER TRIGGERED
